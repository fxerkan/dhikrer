Why it's different

Five things you'll never find inside.

Dhikrer is built on subtraction. What we leave out is the point.

No ads
Not a single banner, interstitial, or "watch to unlock". Ever.
No cost
Free, with no paywalls, subscriptions, or locked features.
No account
No sign-up, no email, no personal information — just open and count.
No tracking
No telemetry. Every count, setting, and statistic lives only on your device.
No permissions
No location, contacts, camera, or storage snooping. No surprises.
Everything you need

A complete tasbih, nothing you don't need.

Fast where it counts, deep where it matters.

Count anywhere

A big tap button, the physical volume keys (even with the screen off), or a resizable home-screen widget.

سبحان الله

The Classic counter

A large ring shows your count from 0–999 with a thousands indicator. Count up or down, and it continues exactly where you left off.

  • Huge tap target, high-contrast number
  • Up or countdown, resume anytime
  • Ring / wave / fill animation effects
الحمد لله

The Tesbih bead-ring

A prayer-bead ring that fills as you count, with divider beads at 33, 66 and 99 — the closest thing to a physical tasbih.

  • 33 / 66 / 99 divider beads
  • Beads light up as the lap fills
  • Per-theme bead shapes & colors
الله أكبر

The Unified button

One giant button fills the screen — the whole surface is the counter. Perfect for eyes-closed, one-thumb counting.

  • Full-screen tap area
  • Shape, size, color & position all yours
  • Gentle haptic on every count
أستغفر الله

Lock mode

Lock everything but the counter. Slip the phone in your pocket and keep counting with the volume keys — no accidental taps, no accidental exits.

  • Only the counter responds
  • Count screen-off with volume keys
  • Prevents pocket miscounts
لا إله إلا الله

My Dhikrs

Your personal library of dhikrs. Add as many as you like, give each its own sound and vibration, then pin, reorder, and swipe to remove.

  • Unlimited saved dhikrs
  • Per-dhikr sound, vibration & goal
  • Pin, reorder, swipe-to-delete
